# Tuning coverage audit

The Samsung handbook asks for systematic tuning across suitable validation folds. The solution has real bounded tuning evidence, but not every frozen champion has a hyperparameter search. No model was retrained during this gate.

| Component | What actually happened | Validation policy / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Seeded Optuna TPE search: **6 trials for energy** and **6 for arrivals**, tuning XGBoost estimators, depth and learning rate. Best validation MAE: 91.8705 kWh and 6.2351 sessions. Baseline and LSTM compared; LSTM best epoch selected on validation. | Chronological July–September 2021 validation; October–December test held out. `team/waseem_demand/reports/optuna.json`, `metrics.json`, `demand/train.py`. |
| Solar | Fixed candidate settings: Seasonal Persistence, Ridge (`alpha=10`), XGBoost (300 estimators, depth 4, learning rate 0.06, subsample/column sample 0.9). **No Optuna or hyperparameter-search record** for frozen solar champions. Family selected separately for 24h/48h by May 2024 chronological validation MAE; June test reported afterward. | `team/mazen_solar/integration/build_handoff.py`, `reports/integration_hardening/selection.json`. |
| EV session risk | CatBoost model settings fixed (450 maximum iterations, depth 6, learning rate 0.06, Balanced class weights); early stopping chose best iteration 10. **No model hyperparameter search evidenced.** A 37-point threshold sweep used validation only; 0.40 selected by F2 with precision at least validation prevalence. | Chronological validation ending 2021-10-16; later test held out. `integration/train.py`, `reports/integration_hardening/threshold_curve.csv`. |
| Grid-load interval | Rule/Isolation Forest settings fixed. The forest has 100 trees, max 256 samples, seed 42. Both score thresholds calibrated to the 99th percentile of natural, unlabeled January–June 2021 validation hours. **No Optuna run**; a bounded deterministic comparison is appropriate for this small proxy screen. | July–December held-out natural and injected sensitivity evidence in `reports/grid_load/comparison.json` and case CSVs. |

**Assessment:** Solution-level systematic tuning is evidenced by Demand's 12 tracked Optuna trials and validation-only threshold selection in two anomaly tasks. Solar and session-risk model hyperparameters are fixed, so the package must not claim comprehensive per-model HPO. The handbook's three-family comparison is demonstrated across demand and solar. No new tuning was run because all models are frozen and further search would risk changing the accepted evaluation story.
