## Data, target and leakage

The raw Jiaxing `Charging_Data.csv` SHA-256 is `6f3643db401327ec4f4a074109987706e421896869acac26a8ee1a5c6e3b45ee`. Re-running the owner cleaning logic reproduced 441,064 cleaned sessions from 441,077 raw rows: five nonpositive intervals and eight overlapping later sessions removed; 92 valid first sessions retained. Cleaning audit counts match owner reports. The rebuilt CSV hash (`87ec74f0fbeea994cf0795b2969a1ba228a64842e7f33e38f2558b82b904c394`) differs from the owner's reported hash, apparently from pandas serialization differences; numeric audit counts matched. The source timezone is not verified.

The target is total session duration in minutes, predicted from start-known fields. `expected_free_time = session_start + predicted_duration`; `expected_wait_minutes = max(0, expected_free_time - as_of)`. End Time is used for historical interval reconstruction and training labels only; it is excluded from inference. Prior charger history uses shifted completed sessions. Candidate occupancy at a historical arrival is reconstructed from recorded intervals, with End Time used only to establish the replay state. This is a retrospective demonstration, not a live-state source. `site_n_chargers` is derived from the full site roster and should be treated as an assumed known capacity; if capacity changed over time it could reveal future site information.

Chronological splits: training 285,225 rows before 2021-07-01; validation 75,767 rows before 2021-10-01; test 80,072 rows from 2021-10-01. Training fits the encoder. Optuna uses three rolling-origin folds within training. The original owner report selected default XGBoost using normal-session validation MAE: median baseline 44.108, Random Forest 43.629, default XGBoost 43.404, tuned XGBoost 43.398 minutes. The tuned gain was below the documented 1% selection threshold. Original champion normal-session test MAE/RMSE were 34.948/83.049 minutes. **The test partition had been scored before tuning**; these are retrospective metrics, not an untouched final estimate.

## Recommendation contract and limits

The deterministic ranker uses weights wait 0.45, historical abnormal-end rate 0.25, demand pressure 0.20 and solar benefit 0.10; lower score wins, with tie-breaking in `ranker.py`. Same-location free chargers are preferred; FAULT, OFFLINE, UNAVAILABLE and RESERVED are excluded. Candidate status, occupancy and history are caller-supplied and must be historical and explicit. The eight-candidate real Jiaxing replay fixture at 12:27:26 recommends charger 8. Missing candidate-level demand and solar inputs use neutral 0.5 constants and `mock_signals_used=true`; these are **not** demand/solar model forecasts. Risk is a training-period abnormal-end rate, not a hardware fault probability. Explanations expose terms and weights; they do not diagnose faults.

The owner's peak replay reports 39,204 held-out arrivals where every arrival had at least one free charger; all compared methods had zero realized wait. Its later-fault proxy and assignment-balance measures do not establish that the ranker improves operations. No current Egyptian station telemetry, confirmed fault labels, real feeder load, or production validation is claimed. The API rejects current dates and future outcomes. The UI uses historical fixtures and displays these limitations.
